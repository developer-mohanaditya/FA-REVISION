## 2. Source Limitations & Disclosures

1. **LIFO Valuation:**
   - Mentioned on Slide 16 of Session 5 as an academic inventory method option.
   - *Status in App:* Explicitly noted as prohibited under French PCG statutory rules; course exercises exclusively employ FIFO and Weighted Average Cost (WAC/CMP).
2. **PP&E Impairment Reversal:**
   - Reversal of current asset impairments (inventory, receivables, securities) is explicitly detailed in Session 6 slides (pp. 14–15).
   - *Status in App:* PP&E impairment is highlighted as theoretically reversible under French GAAP, but noted as source-limited since no multi-year PP&E reversal numeric example was included in Session 4 slides.
3. **No Verbatim Reproduction:**
   - Original exam scenarios (Rex case, Beethoven case) and corrected files were audited to extract problem formats, marking criteria (dc lines), and account numbers. All in-app worked examples use clean original numbers following the exact same accounting principles.
