## Proposed Module 1–8 Mapping

### Key Decision: Session 2+3 split, Session 5+6 merge, Session 6 Part 2 standalone

**Rationale:**
- The "session 2 et 3" PDF (44 pages) covers two logically distinct areas: (a) the double-entry system / accounting process (pages 1–22) and (b) daily operating transactions including VAT and payroll (pages 23–44). These map cleanly to two separate modules.
- Sessions 5 and 6 cover the same topic area (end-of-period entries) across 49 pages combined. Session 5 = adjusting entries + inventory; Session 6 = impairment (current assets) + provisions. They are split into two modules to keep module size manageable.
- Session 6 Part 2 (income tax + profit distribution) is a standalone 16-page deck on a distinct topic. It becomes its own module.
- Session 7 (Cash Flow Statement, 11 pages) is the capstone topic and stands alone.
- This yields exactly 8 modules without inventing content or forcing artificial splits.

## Exam-Training & Quiz Topic Coverage

### Final Quiz 2025–2026 Topics
- **Part 1 (MCQ, 10 questions):** Net income impact, equity/reserves, non-cash items, normal balances, bank T-account reading, VAT collected account, VAT return effects, PP&E NBV calculation, marketable securities impairment, finished goods inventory impact.
- **Part 2 (Rex case):** Journal entries — insurance prepaid, payroll (gross/net/employer), cash withdrawal, capital increase, supplies purchase, revenue with mixed payment, salary entry, discount invoice, loan repayment + interest.
- **Part 3 (Beethoven case):** Forecast financial statements (BS + P&L + CFS) from opening balances + 15 transactions including profit appropriation, receivable collection, payable payment, revenue, purchases, opex, salaries (with social contributions), inventory, PP&E acquisition, disposal, depreciation, interest, loan, income tax.

### Exam Training Handout Topics
- 10 True/False conceptual questions (equity, profit, cash, dividends, depreciation, deferred items, reserves)
- 9 Journal-entry questions: prepaid internet, fixed asset + depreciation, cash withdrawal, capital increase, supplies, revenue with multiple payment modes, payroll, cash discount, loan repayment + interest

### Pattern Summary
- Exam consistently tests: journal entries with correct accounts + amounts, classification decisions, and understanding of statement impacts (not just calculation).
- Three-question format: conceptual, journal-entry exercise, and full-statements exercise.
- Heavy emphasis on: adjustments/cut-off, payroll, VAT, depreciation, impairment.
